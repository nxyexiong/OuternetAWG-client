OuternetAWG Windows client

The build includes dependency license files in licenses\ and versioned module
and NuGet inventories alongside them. Keep that directory with redistributions.

AmneziaWG Go: https://github.com/amnezia-vpn/amneziawg-go
Pinned engine: github.com/amnezia-vpn/amneziawg-go/v3 v3.1.20260828.
Its device, conn and tun packages are compiled directly into the Go service.
No kernel WireGuard driver or official Amnezia UI is used.

WireGuard for Windows: https://git.zx2c4.com/wireguard-windows/
The MIT-licensed winipcfg and firewall helper packages are used for IP Helper
and Windows Filtering Platform integration, not its UI or tunnel engine.

Wintun: https://www.wintun.net/
The unmodified, officially signed Wintun 0.14.1 DLL is redistributed under the
binary license supplied in its archive, reproduced as licenses\Wintun.txt.
Wintun is a trademark of Jason A. Donenfeld.

Avalonia: https://github.com/AvaloniaUI/Avalonia
Avalonia 11.3.22, MIT (Avalonia.txt). The release also contains the .NET 8
runtime; its license and third-party notices are included with the dotnet- prefix.

Microsoft go-winio: https://github.com/microsoft/go-winio
MIT-licensed Windows named-pipe transport.

MicroCom.Runtime 0.11.0: https://github.com/kekekeks/MicroCom
